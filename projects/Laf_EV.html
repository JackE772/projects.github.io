PROJECT / ELECTRONICS

Lafayette EV

This is some of my work on the Lafayette FSAE race car, which is an electric vehicle designed and built by students at Lafayette College. I worked on the safety logic for the car, which involved designing and implementing a PCB that would monitor various sensors and ensure that the car was operating safely. The PCB was designed using KiCad and fabricated by a PCB manufacturer. I also worked on testing and debugging the PCB to ensure that it functioned correctly.

Tools:KiCad

Overview

This project involved several years of work desining and redesining the safety logic for the Lafayette FSAE race car. The safety logic is responsible for monitoring various sensors and ensuring that the car is operating safely. The PCB was designed using KiCad and fabricated by a PCB manufacturer. I also worked on testing and debugging the PCB to ensure that it functioned correctly.

My Contribution

  • Designed top level architecture
  • Created the FPGA design and implemented the logic.
  • Debugged the design and resolved issues.
